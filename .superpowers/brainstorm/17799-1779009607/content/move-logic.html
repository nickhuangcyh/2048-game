Move and Merge Logic Visualization

How should the tiles behave during a move?

A

Simple Slide & Merge

Standard 2048 behavior: tiles slide to the edge, identical neighbors merge once per move.

B

Chain Merge (NOT standard)

Can a single tile merge multiple times in one move? (e.g., [2, 2, 4, 0] -> [8, 0, 0, 0]). Standard 2048 says NO.

C

ID Preservation

Moving tiles keep their ID. Merged tiles get a new ID (or take the ID of one of the parents). Critical for animations.
